Rules

  • All rewards come from a reward vault pre-funded by the project. No new tokens are minted.
  • Each position locks the APR at opening time; later APR changes do not affect existing positions.
  • Rewards accrue linearly by the second, capped at the tier's accrual window (365 days for Flexible).
  • If the reward pool cannot cover a new position's maximum liability, the contract rejects the stake.
  • The contract has no instruction that lets the project withdraw user principal or promised rewards.
